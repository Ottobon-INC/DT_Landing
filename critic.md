Part 1: The Core Positioning Gap (AI Journalist vs. Digital Twin)
1. The Relationship Issue
	Where it’s confusing: The landing page currently presents the AI Journalist and the Digital Twin like two competing features or separate products.
	Why it fails the buyer: A decision-maker gets confused: "Do I need an AI Journalist? Or do I need a Digital Twin? What am I actually buying?"
	The Solution: Explicitly position the AI Journalist as the Engine/Input and the Digital Twin as the Product/Output.
	Hero Reframe: "Your Digital Twin, Powered by the AI Journalist."
	Formula: AI Journalist (Capture) → Intelligence Vault (Structure) → Digital Twin (Execute).
Part 2: Detailed Section-by-Section Critique & Fixes
Section 1: Hero Header & Value Proposition
	Current Copy: "The Knowledge Inside Your Head Is Irreplaceable. And Right Now — It's At Risk."
	Where it’s confusing: It uses negative, fear-based framing ("At Risk"). It sounds like a cybersecurity/backup disaster utility rather than a high-value growth tool.
	The Solution: Flip the narrative from loss prevention to leverage & scalability.
	Option A (Impact-Driven): "Your Decades of Expertise. Now Fully Scalable, Unlimited, and Permanent."
	Option B (Asset-Driven): "Turn Unspoken Intuition Into Your Organization's Greatest Active Asset."
	Sub-headline Fix: "The AI Journalist interviews your top talent to capture tacit intuition and convert it into an active, 24/7 Digital Twin."
Section 2: "How It Works" & Onboarding Flow
	Current Flow: 1. The Interview → 2. Knowledge Extraction → 3. Digital Twin
	Where it’s confusing: The technical architecture terms ("Implicit Data Parsing," "Master Case Mapping," "Deterministic Routing") confuse operational buyers who just want to know how it helps their business.
	The Solution: Simplify the 3-step engine into clear human steps:
	Capture (AI Journalist): Dynamic, interactive dialogue that extracts implicit logic and "gut feelings."
	Structure (Intelligence Core): Automatically turns unspoken intuition into structured decision trees.
	Amplify (Digital Twin): Deploys an active agent to mentor teams, handle complex routing, and scale output.
Section 3: The 90% Implicit Knowledge Breakdown
	Current Copy: "Explicit Knowledge (Documented)" vs. "Implicit Knowledge (Lost)"
	Where it’s confusing: Stating that implicit knowledge "vanishes when you leave" reinforces the negative tone your senior flagged.
	The Solution: Reframe this visual card positively:
	Header: "Capture the 90% That Standard Documentation Misses."
	Explicit Knowledge: "What's written down (Manuals & SOPs) — standard across all companies."
	Implicit Knowledge (Unlocked): "How decisions are actually made (Nuance, Edge Cases, Gut Cues) — your unique competitive advantage."
Section 4: Target Audience Cards (Doctor, Tutor, Recruiter)
	Current Setup: Lists Doctors, Tutors, and Recruiters in the top section and then repeats the exact same three personas under The Digital Twin Impact lower down.
	Where it’s confusing:
	Identity Crisis: B2B enterprise buyers (recruiters, enterprise teams) and B2C/individual creators (tutors) have totally different buying criteria.
	Repetition: Repeating the same personas without adding new depth makes the page feel short on real features.
	The Solution:
	Pick a clear primary audience focus (e.g., Enterprise/Organization-first).
	Replace generic descriptions with concrete ROI metrics:
	Doctor Example: "Reduce Onboarding Time by 60%" — allow junior residents to query 30 years of complex diagnostic edge-cases 24/7.
	Enterprise/Recruiter Example: "Standardize Top-Tier Hiring Quality" — embed your top 1% recruiter’s culture-fit intuition into every hiring pipeline.
Section 5: The "Retirement / Offboarding" Trigger Section
	Current Copy: "You could retire tomorrow. If you did, decades of intuition... would walk out the door with you."
	Where it’s confusing: Sounds defensive, punitive, and heavy.
	The Solution: Shift to legacy and institutional memory.
	New Headline: "Build a Cognitive Legacy That Multiplies Your Impact."
	New Sub-headline: "Ensure your problem-solving framework continues to guide, mentor, and execute across the entire organization forever."
Part 3: What Is Still Missing from the Page (Buyer Checklist)
To convert high-level enterprise prospects, the landing page needs answers to these 3 missing buyer questions:
	Deployment / Where does the Twin live?
	Add a visual or short callout showing how teams interact with the Twin (e.g., Slack/Teams Integration, Interactive Chat Portal, or API Decision Engine).
	Data Privacy & IP Ownership:
	Add a single trust banner: "100% Enterprise Encrypted. Your proprietary intuition models remain fully private and owned by your organization."
	Static vs. Dynamic Learning:
	Clarify whether the Twin stays frozen after the interview or continuously refines its models as new cases are processed.
